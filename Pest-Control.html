Pest & Disease Control

Protect your crops using safe, effective, and sustainable pest management practices.

Common Crop Pests

Know the signs and solutions for common agricultural pests.

Aphids

Small insects that suck plant sap and weaken crops. Use neem oil or insecticidal soap.

Caterpillars

Feed on leaves and stems. Hand-pick or use biological pesticides.

Locusts

Can destroy large crop areas quickly. Monitor fields and apply approved control measures.

Prevention Tips

  • ✔ Inspect crops weekly.
  • ✔ Remove infected plants.
  • ✔ Rotate crops every season.
  • ✔ Keep fields weed-free.
  • ✔ Encourage beneficial insects.
  • ✔ Avoid overwatering.
  • ✔ Use disease-resistant seeds.
  • ✔ Apply organic pesticides.
  • ✔ Monitor humidity.
  • ✔ Store harvested crops properly.
