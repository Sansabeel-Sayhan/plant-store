Cultivation Process

🌾 Rice Cultivation

  • Prepare wet field.
  • Sow quality seeds.
  • Maintain irrigation.
  • Apply fertilizers.
  • Harvest after maturity.

🌽 Wheat Cultivation

  • Plough the land.
  • Sow certified seeds.
  • Irrigate regularly.
  • Remove weeds.
  • Harvest when golden.
